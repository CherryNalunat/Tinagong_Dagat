Discover Tinagong Dagat

Tinagong Dagat comes alive in various shades of emerald and deep blue depending on the weather. Located high up in the mountains of Barangay Ongyod, this highland freshwater oasis is famous among local trekkers for its peaceful atmosphere, rolling green hills, and misty mountain horizons.
